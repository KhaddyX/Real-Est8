How We'll Work Together

1

Get to Know You

We'll discuss your must-haves, budget, timeline, and what matters most to you in a home. This isn't a sales pitch—it's a conversation about your goals and how I can help you achieve them.

2

Find Your Home

I'll send you listings that match your criteria, including off-market opportunities you won't find online. We'll tour homes together, and I'll point out both the positives and potential concerns so you can make an informed decision.

3

Make Your Offer

I'll help you craft a competitive offer and negotiate on your behalf to get you the best price and terms. My goal is to protect your interests while keeping the deal moving forward.

4

Close with Confidence

From inspections to appraisals to final walkthrough, I'll guide you through every step and handle any issues that arise. You'll never feel alone in the process—I'm with you until you have the keys in hand.

1

Home Valuation

I'll analyze recent sales, current market conditions, and your home's unique features to determine the optimal listing price. Pricing right from the start is crucial to attracting serious buyers and maximizing your profit.

Prepare & Market

Professional photography, compelling listing descriptions, and strategic marketing across MLS, social media, and my network of buyers. I'll also provide staging advice to help your home show its best.

4

Smooth Closing

I'll manage every detail from contract to closing, coordinating with inspectors, appraisers, and the buyer's agent. You'll know exactly what's happening at every stage, and I'll handle any challenges that come up.
